description: "Move your products, checkout, license keys and subscribers from Lemon Squeezy to Vatly before 31 March 2027."
---

Lemon Squeezy stops processing payments on **31 March 2027**. This guide covers the technical side of moving to Vatly. Not sure yet whether Vatly fits your business? Start with [Moving from Lemon Squeezy](https://vatly.com/move-from-lemon-squeezy) on our website.

Keep selling on Lemon Squeezy until your Vatly checkout is live.

## 1. Start in test mode

Grab your test API key in the dashboard under **Developers**. Test mode works right away, before any review. To go live you need a Mollie account (connect yours or open one during onboarding), the URLs of your terms and privacy policy, and your products.

## 2. Recreate your products

Add your products and subscription plans in the dashboard under **Manage Products**, or through the API. Most stores have a handful, so this is usually quicker than scripting it. Vatly reviews every product before it goes on sale, because Vatly is the legal seller. A review typically takes one to two business days.

Live payments are in EUR today. You can already test USD in test mode.

## 3. Connect your checkout

Point your buy buttons at a Vatly hosted checkout. Use the [API](/api-reference/checkouts), the [PHP SDK](/packages/php/readme), the [Laravel package](/packages/laravel/readme) or one of the [integrations](/integrations). On Laravel, [Migrating from Cashier](/packages/laravel/migrating-to-vatly) shows how to run Vatly next to the Lemon Squeezy package while you move.

Vatly offers a hosted checkout only, so replace any checkout overlay with a link or redirect.

Then listen to [webhooks](/guides/webhooks) to grant and revoke access in your app.

## 4. Issue license keys from webhooks

Vatly doesn't issue license keys. Keep them in your own app or in a dedicated licensing service, so they never have to move when you change payment provider again:

- create a key on `order.paid`
- revoke it on `subscription.canceled_immediately` or `refund.completed`

Your customers never need another vendor login.

## 5. Go live

Once Vatly has approved your products and Mollie has verified your business, switch to your live API key. From then on, send every new purchase through Vatly.

## 6. Move your existing subscribers

A subscriber's payment details belong to their current seller of record, so no provider can move active subscriptions for you. Each subscriber re-subscribes once, at Vatly:

- Email each subscriber shortly before their next Lemon Squeezy renewal, with a link to a Vatly checkout for the same plan. They've already paid for the current period, so switching then means nobody pays twice.
- Once they've re-subscribed, cancel their Lemon Squeezy subscription at the end of its current period.
- Annual subscribers who renew after 31 March 2027 need to move before then.

## 7. Export your Lemon Squeezy data

Before 31 March 2027, export your customers, orders and subscriptions from Lemon Squeezy. Lemon Squeezy's [migration page](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/migration) explains what you can export. Keep the export: your past invoices stay with Lemon Squeezy as the seller of those sales.
