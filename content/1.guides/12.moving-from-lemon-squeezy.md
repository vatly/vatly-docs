Lemon Squeezy closed new sign-ups on 16 November 2026 and stops processing payments on **31 March 2027**. Every store needs a new home before then.

The default path is [Stripe Managed Payments](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/migration), which Lemon Squeezy's own migration tooling points to. It's a fine choice for many stores. But you have to move anyway, so it's worth choosing your next Merchant of Record on purpose, not just following the tooling.

Vatly is the European Merchant of Record for AI, software and digital products. We're headquartered in the Netherlands, keep your data in the EU and run on Mollie. We're younger than Lemon Squeezy, so not every feature is here yet. This page says plainly what is, what's coming and what isn't, so you can decide quickly.

## Is Vatly a good fit?

Vatly fits if:

- your business is headquartered in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland
- you sell software, AI products or digital products as subscriptions, one-off purchases or prepaid credit packs
- you want your seller of record, and your customer data, in Europe

Vatly is not the right home if you need a marketplace with multiple sellers, usage-based or metered billing, or an affiliate program run by your payment provider.

### Why license keys stay with you

Your license keys are part of your product, not part of your payments. If they live at your Merchant of Record, you have to migrate them every time you change providers, which is exactly what makes leaving Lemon Squeezy hard.

Keep keys in your own app or in a dedicated licensing service, and issue them from Vatly's [webhooks](/guides/webhooks). For example, create a key on `order.paid` and revoke it on `subscription.canceled_immediately` or `refund.completed`. Your customers never need another vendor login.

## Your timeline

Work back from 31 March 2027:

1. **Now.** Sign up and build your integration in test mode.
2. **December.** Recreate your products and get them approved. A product review typically takes one to two business days.
3. **Mid-January.** Go live, and send every new customer to your Vatly checkout. Lemon Squeezy recommends starting your migration by then too.
4. **January to March.** Move your existing subscribers.
5. **31 March 2027.** Lemon Squeezy stops processing payments. Anyone still on it stops renewing.

Keep selling on Lemon Squeezy until your Vatly checkout is live.

## Move step by step

### 1. Sign up and start in test mode

[Create your account](https://my.vatly.com) and grab your test API keys. Test mode works right away, before any review. To go live you need a Mollie account (connect yours or open one during onboarding), the URLs of your terms and privacy policy, and your products.

### 2. Recreate your products

Add your products and subscription plans in the dashboard or through the API. Most stores have a handful, so this is usually quicker than scripting it. Vatly reviews every product before it goes on sale, because Vatly is the legal seller.

### 3. Connect your checkout

Point your buy buttons at a Vatly hosted checkout. Use the [API](/api-reference/checkouts), the [PHP SDK](/packages/php/readme), the [Laravel package](/packages/laravel/readme) or one of the [integrations](/integrations). On Laravel, [Migrating from Cashier](/packages/laravel/migrating-to-vatly) shows how to run Vatly next to the Lemon Squeezy package while you move.

Then listen to [webhooks](/guides/webhooks) to grant and revoke access in your app.

### 4. Go live and send new customers to Vatly

Once Vatly has approved your products and Mollie has verified your business, switch to your live keys. From then on, every new purchase goes through Vatly.

### 5. Move your existing subscribers

A subscriber's payment details belong to their current seller of record, so no provider can move active subscriptions for you. Each subscriber re-subscribes once, at Vatly. To make that painless:

- Email each subscriber shortly before their next Lemon Squeezy renewal, with a link to a Vatly checkout for the same plan. They've already paid for the current period, so switching then means nobody pays twice.
- Once they've re-subscribed, cancel their Lemon Squeezy subscription at the end of its current period.
- Annual subscribers who renew after 31 March 2027 need to move before then.

### 6. Export your Lemon Squeezy data

Before 31 March 2027, export your customers, orders and subscriptions from Lemon Squeezy. Lemon Squeezy's [migration page](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/migration) explains what you can export. Keep the export: your past invoices stay with Lemon Squeezy as the seller of those sales.
