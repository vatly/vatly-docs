**Vatly is the European alternative to Lemon Squeezy and to Stripe Managed Payments, the service Lemon Squeezy is moving its merchants to.** Vatly is a Merchant of Record headquartered in the Netherlands, built on Mollie, and keeps your data in the EU. Vatly is the legal seller on every sale and handles payments, VAT and compliance, for 5% + €0.50 per transaction with no surcharges.

Lemon Squeezy stops processing payments, including subscription renewals, on **31 March 2027**, and recommends starting your migration by mid-January 2027. This guide explains how to move to Vatly without interrupting your sales.

## Why choose a European alternative after Lemon Squeezy?

Lemon Squeezy points its merchants to Stripe Managed Payments. If you're a European business, you may prefer a Merchant of Record that is European itself:

- **EU-based seller.** Vatly is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates under EU law. Lemon Squeezy and Stripe Managed Payments are provided by a US company.
- **EU data.** Vatly is EU-hosted and processes merchant and customer data in the EU, under GDPR.
- **European payment methods.** iDEAL | Wero, Bancontact and SEPA Direct Debit next to cards and Apple Pay, through Mollie.
- **A second provider.** Already on Stripe or moving there? Vatly can run next to it, so your revenue doesn't depend on one provider.
- **Hands-on help.** We help you plan and run the move.

For a side-by-side comparison, see [Vatly vs Lemon Squeezy](https://vatly.com/vs/lemonsqueezy).

## Is Vatly a fit?

Vatly works for businesses headquartered in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland that sell AI, software or digital products. Check these before you start:

- **Payment methods.** Vatly supports cards, Apple Pay and European methods such as iDEAL | Wero, Bancontact and SEPA Direct Debit. We're working with PayPal to enable it.
- **Currencies.** We're adding multi-currency support, starting with USD.
- **Delivery.** Vatly doesn't deliver files or license keys. See [License keys and file delivery](#license-keys-and-file-delivery).

## 1. Export your data from Lemon Squeezy

Download your customers, orders and subscriptions using Lemon Squeezy's [export guide](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/orders/exporting-orders). You'll use the subscription export to see who renews when.

## 2. Set up your products in Vatly

Create a [one-off product](/api-reference/one-off-products) or [subscription plan](/api-reference/subscription-plans) in Vatly for each Lemon Squeezy product or variant you still sell.

## 3. Run Lemon Squeezy and Vatly side by side

You don't have to switch everything on one day:

- Point your website, pricing page and in-app upgrade flows at Vatly checkouts, so new customers buy through Vatly.
- Leave existing subscriptions on Lemon Squeezy for now. They keep renewing there until you move them.
- Handle webhooks from both providers until the last Lemon Squeezy subscription has ended.

::tip
**Using Laravel?** Read [Migrating from Cashier](/packages/laravel/migrating-to-vatly). It shows how to run Vatly next to the Lemon Squeezy package in the same app, including both webhook endpoints and moving from the Lemon Squeezy overlay to Vatly's checkout.
::

## 4. Move your existing subscribers

You can start this right away: invite each subscriber to subscribe on Vatly, then cancel their Lemon Squeezy subscription.

- Send them a [Vatly checkout](/api-reference/checkouts) for their plan. To avoid charging twice, set `trialDays` so the trial runs until their paid Lemon Squeezy period ends.
- Depending on the payment method, the customer may pay a small amount to verify it. The checkout shows the amount, and whether it's refunded, before they pay.
- Once their Vatly subscription is active, cancel the Lemon Squeezy subscription.
- Consider giving them a reason to switch early, rather than waiting for the deadline.
- Tell customers why they're moving and what will change on their bank statement.

**PayPal subscribers:** Lemon Squeezy can't transfer PayPal subscriptions to any provider, so these customers need to subscribe again.

**Moving saved cards:** we're working with Mollie on importing existing card subscriptions from Lemon Squeezy, so card subscribers don't have to re-enter their details. If you have many subscribers, contact us before you start.

## License keys and file delivery

Lemon Squeezy delivered license keys and files for you. With Vatly you deliver them yourself, in one of two ways:

- **Use a licensing or delivery service**, such as Keygen, connected through Vatly webhooks.
- **Build it yourself.** Listen for `order.paid` to issue a license key or send a download link, and for subscription events such as `subscription.canceled_for_nonpayment` to revoke access. See [Webhooks](/guides/webhooks).

- Your help pages explain where customers manage billing and find invoices.
- Your last Lemon Squeezy payout has arrived.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best European alternative to Lemon Squeezy?

Vatly is a European Merchant of Record for AI, software and digital products, headquartered in the Netherlands and built on Mollie. Like Lemon Squeezy, Vatly is the legal seller on every sale and handles payments and VAT. Unlike Lemon Squeezy, it's an EU company and keeps your data in the EU.

### Is Lemon Squeezy shutting down?

Yes. Lemon Squeezy closed new sign-ups on 16 November 2026 and stops processing payments, including subscription renewals, on 31 March 2027. The deadline won't be extended.

### Do I have to move to Stripe Managed Payments?

No. Lemon Squeezy recommends Stripe Managed Payments, but you can move to any provider. If you'd rather sell through a European Merchant of Record, you can move to Vatly.

### What's the European alternative to Stripe Managed Payments?

Vatly. Stripe Managed Payments is provided by a US company. Vatly is a Dutch Merchant of Record that processes your data in the EU and offers European payment methods such as iDEAL | Wero, Bancontact and SEPA Direct Debit.

### Can I use Vatly next to Stripe Managed Payments?

Yes. Many businesses use two providers to spread risk. You can sell some products or markets through Vatly and keep others on Stripe.

### Can my Lemon Squeezy subscribers move to Vatly?

Yes. You can invite them to subscribe on Vatly today, with a trial until their paid Lemon Squeezy period ends. We're also working with Mollie on importing card subscriptions directly.

### Does Vatly support PayPal and USD?

We're working with PayPal to enable it, and we're adding multi-currency support, starting with USD.
