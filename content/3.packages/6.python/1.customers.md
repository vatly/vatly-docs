title: "Customers"
description: "Vatly Python SDK - Customers"
---

`client.customers` provides `create`, `get`, `update`, `list`, `list_all`,
`create_portal_session`, `list_subscriptions`, `list_all_subscriptions`, and
`get_subscription`. Filter by email via `ListCustomersParams(email=...)` or
`{"email": "person@example.com"}`. Email identity is storefront- and mode-scoped.

`update` changes identity name/email, not billing-address details. Portal sessions
are short-lived and single-use: redirect to the returned `url`; do not store it as
a permanent link. Pass `{}` for an empty portal-session request body.

See [method inventory](/packages/python/api#customers) and [customer fields](https://docs.vatly.com/api-reference/customers).
