## Framework packages

Using a framework rather than an app builder? Official [Laravel](/packages/laravel/readme) and [Django](/packages/django/readme) packages integrate Vatly with your application. Their installation, configuration and API guides live under [Packages](/packages), alongside the SDKs and fluent packages, rather than being duplicated here.

These packages are in alpha. Every alpha release may include breaking changes. Pin exact versions during alpha.

## Connecting another app?

Start with [Build your own integration](/guides/build-your-own-integration) to understand what Vatly handles, what your app needs, and what to ask your developer or agent to build. It also helps you choose a fluent package, SDK, or direct API approach.

If you are ready for your first API request, follow the [Quickstart](/guides/quickstart). Implementation examples live in the [package docs](/packages), with exact endpoint and field details in the [API reference](/api-reference).
